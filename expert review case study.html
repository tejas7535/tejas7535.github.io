01 / Challenge & Strategy

Problem & Central Solution

Transitioning from reactive, subjective reviews to a structured, repeatable audit pipeline.

The Problem Unstructured Inquiries

Frequent, Ad-hoc UX Audit Requests

Product Owners, SaaS tool managers, and stakeholders frequently request expert reviews from the Central UX Team without structured context or clear objectives.

Inconsistent Evaluations & Feedback

Without standardized criteria, UX audits were subject to reviewer bias, producing scattered findings that made prioritization difficult for engineering teams.

The Solution Unified Framework

PO Kick-off & Alignment Templates

A structured questionnaire tailored for stakeholders to define target users, critical tasks, performance metrics, and business value before conducting audits.

Universal UX Evaluation Sheets

Modular sheets based on 6 core UX pillars with a 0–4 severity scale to quantify usability risks and output actionable recommendations.

📋 Kick off - PO

Target: Product Owners Phase: Intake & Alignment
Who are main user groups?
  • Who is using the application / tool? (overall target audience)
  • Do primary personas exist?
  • Where are users geographically located?
  • Are users internal, external, or both?
  • How many total active users exist?
Answer
Is the product designed around a well understood user need?
  • Can you show observations / evidence for user need?
  • What evidence do you have to support tool necessity?
  • Have users explicitly requested this tool/feature?
  • Is it compliant with usage standards?
Answer
What are our users' main tasks and decisions that have to be taken?
  • What are the top 3 highest workload tasks?
  • What key decisions are made while navigating the tool?
  • Can users execute error-prone or critical fail operations?
  • What background expert knowledge are users expected to possess?
Answer
What are the main goals and values for our users?
  • What qualitative and quantitative outcomes must be fulfilled?
  • Why do users choose this specific solution over alternatives?
Answer
Is there a direct business value tied to the solution?
  • How does feature delivery drive direct business value impact?
  • What business metrics or KPIs are targeted for improvement?
  • How does it increase margin or optimize time savings?
